02 Springs that last

Up to 5× the life of a standard spring.

A spring's life is measured in cycles — one open and close. The minimum spec is 10,000 cycles; most springs last 10,000–20,000. We install springs rated up to 50,000 cycles.

Minimum spec10,000 cycles
Typical spring10,000 – 20,000 cycles
Garage Door ASAP springsup to 50,000 cycles

Why replace both springs?

When one breaks, the other carries double the load and usually fails soon after. Replacing both saves a second visit.

Never DIY a spring

Springs are under extreme tension. Without the right tools and procedures, they can seriously injure you or damage property.

03 Real work

Off track to back on — same day.

An off-track door isn't just ugly — it's unstable and dangerous. Never run it by hand or with the opener. Here's what our technicians do:

  1. 01Find the cause — broken cables, bent tracks or tracks pulling off the wall.
  2. 02Adjust or straighten the tracks and fix the roller alignment.
  3. 03Clean the tracks and lubricate with commercial-grade oil.
  4. 04Final assessment so no component is missing or worn.
