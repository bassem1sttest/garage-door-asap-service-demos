05FAQ

Good to know.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

01

Why replace both springs if only one broke?

+

The other spring has been carrying double the load and usually fails soon after. Replacing both saves you a second visit.

02

Can I replace a spring myself?

+

Please don't. Springs are under extreme tension and can seriously injure you without the right tools and procedures.

03

Should rollers be replaced with the springs?

+

It's recommended. Most rollers last about 10,000 cycles, roughly the same as a standard spring.

04

My door is off track. Can I still use it?

+

No. An off-track door is unstable. Don't move it by hand or with the opener until it's repaired.
