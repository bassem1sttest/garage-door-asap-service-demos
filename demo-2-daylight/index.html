        FAQ

        Questions, answered.

        Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

        +

        Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including weekends and holidays. If your door is stuck open or won't close, call (877) 787-7121 any time.

        Why replace both springs if only one broke?

        +

        When one spring breaks, the other has been carrying double the pressure and usually fails shortly after. Replacing both at once saves you a second service call.

        Should rollers be replaced with the springs?

        +

        It's recommended. Most rollers last around 10,000 cycles — about the same as a standard spring. Worn rollers also put extra strain on your opener and shorten its life.

        Can I replace a spring myself?

        +

        Please don't. Springs are under extreme tension, and without the right tools and safety procedures you could seriously hurt yourself, your family or your property.

        Repair or replace my opener?

        +

        Sometimes a repair is the cost-effective choice; sometimes a new opener is. Our technician evaluates the opener, lays out every option and gives an honest recommendation.
